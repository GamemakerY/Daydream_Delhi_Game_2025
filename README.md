# BloodOath

![BloodOath Banner](path/to/banner-image.png)

## Overview

**BloodOath** is a narrative-driven action RPG about loyalty, sacrifice, and destiny. Two warriors swear a blood oath to rescue the captured heir of a fallen kingdom — but a deadly curse means only one may survive the journey. The choices you make shape the fate of the heir, the warriors, and the kingdom itself.

---

## Story

The kingdom has fallen. The heir is taken by a mysterious cult. Bound by a blood oath, two protectors set out on a cursed path where only one may reach the end. Along the way, they face trials that test their trust, love, and sacrifice. In the final confrontation, you must choose who lives — and who dies — changing the course of the story forever.

---

## Features

- Dual protagonist gameplay with unique combat styles  
- Deep narrative with branching choices and emotional stakes     
- Rich lore and character-driven storytelling
